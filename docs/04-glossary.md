# Glossary

- **silverton_sku**: Our local part number and the permanent identity of a physical part. Format `CATEGORY-COLOR[-SIZE]-SUFFIX`. Generated by the database, never reused or changed. Used for fulfillment.
- **Category code**: 3-letter code for the kind of part (e.g. HNG). First segment of the SKU.
- **SKU suffix**: 4 random characters, unique across all parts, avoiding look-alike characters (0, 1, I, L, O).
- **Alias**: Any outside number that identifies a part (manufacturer, supplier, OEM, legacy, barcode).
- **Source**: Who issued an alias: a factory, supplier, OEM, or us.
- **Supplier part**: A buying relationship: part + supplier + their part number + terms.
- **Product**: A furniture product. Every variant is its own product; products share nothing except parts.
- **Fitment**: A record that a part fits a product (`part_fits_product`). May be missing or incomplete.
- **Assembly letter**: Up to 2 letters or digits (usually a single letter) identifying the part in a product's assembly instructions. Belongs to the part-product pair; unique within a product.
- **Profile**: A user's record with a unique username (from their email), used for created_by and updated_by.
- **System profile**: Stand-in author for imports and other changes made without a signed-in user.
- **Movement**: One immutable record of stock changing (receive, move, issue, adjust).
- **Stock**: Current quantity of a part at a location, derived from movements.
- **On hand**: Sum of stock for a part across all locations.
- **Idempotency key**: Client-generated unique value so a retried submit is not applied twice.
- **UoM**: Unit of measure (each, pair, set, bag of 10).
- **Cycle count**: Counting actual stock and adjusting the system to match.
- **Superseded**: A part replaced by a newer part; points to its replacement.
